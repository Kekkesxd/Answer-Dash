HOW TO PLAY

Move with WASD or ARROW KEYS

Read the question and run to the correct answer zone

Landing on the wrong answer or running out of time costs you a life.

Red obstacles block your path — navigate around them.

Orange obstacles (!) will teleport you back to the center!

You have a limited amount of time per question — watch the timer!

Press LET'S GO when you're ready.
